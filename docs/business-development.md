# Proposed business development strategy

## Objective
Build predictable support for free services while increasing delivery capacity and access. Growth should be paced against service quality and volunteer availability.

## Development priorities
| Priority | Audience and offer | Proposed approach | Success evidence | Main dependency |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Recurring giving | Individual supporters seeking sustained impact | Explain a defined program need, provide an opt-in recurring gift route and periodic updates | Active recurring donors, retention, net receipts | Approved payment channel and donor administration |
| Institutional partnerships | Universities and education networks | Propose a small workshop, resource-sharing or referral pilot | Signed scope, completed pilot, participant feedback | Named partner owner and delivery capacity |
| Corporate sponsorship | Employers and education-related businesses | Offer a specific program with a clear budget and agreed recognition | Qualified opportunities, signed agreements, receipts | Approved benefits and realistic commitments |
| Grant development | Funders whose published scope matches the mission | Screen eligibility, prepare an evidence register and submit tailored proposals | Eligible applications, decisions, funding received | Verified status, budget and application requirements |
| Volunteer recruitment | Relevant professional and student networks | Recruit for specific expertise gaps and train before assignment | Onboarded qualified volunteers and sustained participation | Training and coordinator capacity |
| Community outreach | Relevant communities and trusted referral partners | Explain eligibility, services, boundaries and expected response process | Eligible requests progressing to completed services | Intake readiness and channel attribution |

No named funder, partner eligibility or current opportunity is verified in this project. Research those details before execution.

## Partnership pipeline
Use: identified -> qualified -> discovery -> proposal -> agreement -> pilot -> review -> renew/close. Record stage-entry date, next action, proposed owner, fit, delivery commitments and closure reason. A conversation is not a confirmed partnership; a pledge is not cash received.

Qualification questions: Does the opportunity advance the mission? Is the beneficiary group relevant? Can ZAN deliver the commitment? Are costs funded? Are data-sharing and recognition terms acceptable? What evidence will determine renewal?

## Outreach brief
Prepare a one-page brief containing the beneficiary need, proposed activity, intended outputs, evaluation plan, delivery responsibility, budget placeholders and specific request. Use verified achievements only. Obtain permission before sharing beneficiary stories or identifying details. Outreach text is a draft; nothing is sent by this project.

## Prioritization
Score each opportunity from 1 to 5 for mission fit, expected beneficiary value, capacity fit and funding durability. Separately rate effort and uncertainty. Discuss scores rather than interpreting them as measured precision. Defer opportunities without a delivery owner or with unresolved commitments.

## Financial controls
Approve commitments before delivery; document restricted funding conditions; separate pledged, received and refunded amounts; retain evidence of allowable spending. Determine reporting currency before aggregating donations. Do not subtract costs twice when interpreting fundraising results.

## Review cadence
A proposed monthly development review considers pipeline movement, committed delivery burden, upcoming deadlines and funding concentration. Continue, redesign or close each initiative based on evidence. Baselines and numeric targets remain TBD until records and capacity are reviewed.
