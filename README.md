# ZAN: Business Model, Operations and KPI Governance

A documentation-only portfolio project translating ZAN's reference business model into a proposed operating framework, business development strategy and consistent performance standards.

**Status:** Proposed framework for review. No operational dataset, measured results, approved organizational policies or implemented integrations are included. Role assignments refer to proposed responsibilities, not appointments. Prepared 2 November 2026.

## ZAN online

[Website](https://www.zan.foundation/) | [Instagram: @foundation.zan](https://www.instagram.com/foundation.zan/)

The Instagram link and logo were supplied by the project requester. Branding identifies the organization discussed; the proposed framework is not presented as an officially approved ZAN policy. The logo remains the property of its respective rights holder.

## Purpose
Help a volunteer-led organization connect its mission to repeatable services, clear accountability and evidence-based decisions. The reference material describes support for Iranian and Afghan women pursuing education and careers through free mentorship, application reviews, workshops and scholarship resources.

## Intended outcomes
A shared language for service performance; a documented applicant-to-volunteer workflow; clear quality controls; and a prioritized, mission-aligned growth plan. These are intended outcomes, not achieved benefits.

## How to use
1. Review the source register and resolve mission, eligibility and ownership questions.
2. Walk through the operating process with coordinators and volunteers.
3. Approve KPI definitions using worked examples before collecting or reporting values.
4. Assign role owners and select the first roadmap initiatives.
5. Use the templates to record decisions and changes.

All documents are Markdown and can be read directly on GitHub. No software installation or dataset is required. Publish only materials ZAN authorizes for public release; the original PDFs and personal records are not bundled.

## Contribution process
Propose edits through a pull request stating the problem, affected process or metric, proposed wording and downstream effects. Obtain review from the relevant role owner. Record approved definition changes with an effective date; do not silently change historical calculations.
