# Proposed implementation roadmap

Timing is relative to a future approved project start. Named roles are proposed; no task has been assigned to a person.

|---|---|---|---|---|

Data-dependent measurement remains deferred until appropriate records exist. Document completion is not evidence of operational improvement.

## Lean improvement case: reduce review delays
**Define:** Reduce avoidable waiting from accepted request to delivery while protecting quality and volunteer sustainability. Scope one service type before expanding.

**Measure:** Future baseline needs stage timestamps, original deadlines, workload and quality checks. Validate definitions first. No current baseline exists.

**Analyze:** Map queues and handoffs; investigate incomplete intake, expertise gaps, overloaded reviewers and rework. Use evidence to distinguish frequent causes from memorable anecdotes.

**Improve:** A proposed pilot could combine a completeness checklist, visible capacity register and explicit assignment acceptance. Select interventions after baseline review rather than assuming these are proven fixes.

**Control:** Track turnaround, on-time delivery, first-pass quality, complaints and volunteer workload. Pause or revise the pilot if quality or workload worsens under agreed rules. Retain change history and review evidence before scaling.

## Six-month outlook
If initial controls work, standardize coordinator training, consolidate reports and pilot one partnership. Confirm sustainable capacity before expanding outreach. Determine whether a simple shared register meets operational needs before commissioning automation.

## One-to-three-year outlook
Consider broader services, more durable funding and technology investment only after demand, access, quality, costs and maintainability are understood. Source proposals for AI matching, mobile tools or paid services remain options, not commitments [S1, pp. 3-5].

## Risk register
| Risk | Proposed mitigation | Proposed owner |
|---|---|---|
| Outreach exceeds service capacity | Gate campaigns against availability and backlog | Operations |
| KPI ambiguity creates conflicting reports | Dictionary, worked examples and change approval | Metric steward |
| Volunteer burnout | Availability review, workload visibility and pause process | Volunteer coordinator |
| Outcome claims overstate impact | Report missing outcomes and attribution limits | Impact lead |
| Funding concentration | Diversify pipeline and monitor commitments versus receipts | Development lead |
| Sensitive information becomes public | Minimize collection, restrict access and approve publication | Responsible leadership role |
| Automation is adopted before processes stabilize | Require process readiness and a feasibility decision | Operations / technical role |
